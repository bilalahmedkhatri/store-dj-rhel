Acceptable Use Policy

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") outlines the prohibited activities for any merchant or user utilizing the GoPayFast payment gateway provided by e-sahulat. Failure to comply may result in immediate suspension or termination of services.

Prohibited Merchants & Activities

You may not use our services for any transactions involving:

Illegal Products & Services

Illegal drugs, narcotics, prescription medicines without valid authorization, or any items prohibited by the laws of Pakistan.

Dangerous Goods

Weapons, ammunition, explosives, fireworks, or toxic/radioactive materials.

Financial & Legal Services

Ponzi schemes, pyramid selling, multi-level marketing (MLM), unlicensed money transmission, or currency exchange (Hawala/Hundi).

Adult Content

Pornography, sexually explicit materials, or escort services.

Gambling

Online casinos, sports betting, or any unlicensed gambling activities.

Merchant Responsibilities

  • Clearly display product prices and refund policies.
  • Obtain necessary licenses for regulated businesses.
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions through proper customer verification.

Enforcement: We regularly audit merchant transactions. Accounts found violating this AUP will be blocked, and pending settlements may be frozen for investigation.
